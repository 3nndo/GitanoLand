The casino is the MineVegas mod: roulette, blackjack, poker, slot machine, fortune wheel, Plinko and duck derby. Here EVERYTHING is played with Kirkys: you bet Kirkys and win Kirkys.
<NEWLINE>
To build one, read the casino guide (crafting recipe) and hire a croupier in a village.
<NEWLINE>
Operators can tune the bets, payouts and odds of each machine with the Croupier's Hand.
<NEWLINE>
And if a casino feels like a scam... there is the ATTACK.
